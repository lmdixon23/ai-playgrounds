AI Playgrounds · Activity Packs

Ready-to-assign inquiry labs

These pilot activities turn an interactive applet into a complete learner task: predict → run → observe → explain → transfer. Student responses stay in the browser and can be printed. No account or backend is required.

NN-1 · Core lab · 35–45 min

Make it fail, then make it learn

Use the Tiny Neural Network to investigate linearity, hidden-layer capacity, training dynamics, and generalization by deliberately creating both failure and success cases.

Recommended after an introduction to neurons, activations, loss, and train/test data.

Open NN-1

CNN-1 · Core lab · 40–50 min

Be the filter

Use the Convolution Playground to connect hand-calculated multiply-and-sum arithmetic to edge detection, learned filters, and pooling.

Recommended after NN-1 or a short introduction to image grids and convolution kernels.

Open CNN-1

Teacher-use boundary

These public pages are student-facing. Teacher answer keys and grading exemplars are intentionally not published on the public site. The current Activity Pack pilot is English-only; the core applets remain multilingual.
