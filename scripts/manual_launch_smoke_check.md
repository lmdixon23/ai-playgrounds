# Manual launch smoke check

Record tester, date, release, browser/device, and PASS/FAIL evidence for:

- Signed-out cache-busted landing-page load.
- One physical phone in portrait and landscape.
- Keyboard-only landing-page path and two representative applets.
- Practical NVDA, VoiceOver, or equivalent screen-reader pass.
- Student Lab and Teacher Pack print/PDF export.
- Analytics opt-out, DNT/GPC, canonical-host gate, and offline no-request behaviour.
- Three campaign-source events and absence of arbitrary query content in analytics.

This is a smoke check, not accessibility conformance certification.
