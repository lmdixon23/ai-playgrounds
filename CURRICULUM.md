# Curriculum guide

AI Playgrounds has **15 applets: 13 Foundations/course-track labs and 2 Modern AI extensions**. The [interactive Curriculum Map](https://lmdixon23.github.io/ai-playgrounds/curriculum.html) and [Teacher Pack](https://lmdixon23.github.io/ai-playgrounds/teacher-pack.html) provide topic navigation and classroom links.

## Foundations and modern extensions

The Foundations track covers pathfinding, local search, logical agents, SAT, Bayesian updating and networks, nearest neighbors, overfitting, neural networks, clustering, convolution, reinforcement learning, and adversarial search. The Modern AI extensions inspect Transformer language modeling and agent tool use/context protocols.

The course order below is a navigation aid, not a claim that these applets form a complete course or satisfy a particular syllabus. Prerequisites and teaching time depend on the class. Each lab exposes a bounded mechanism with explicit limitations.

## Assignment layers

- **Level 1:** every lab has one active, stable 10–15 minute Quick Assign built around prediction, action, observation, explanation, and transfer.
- **Level 2:** [NN-1](https://lmdixon23.github.io/ai-playgrounds/activities/nn-1.html) and [CNN-1](https://lmdixon23.github.io/ai-playgrounds/activities/cnn-1.html) provide longer, connected classroom experiments. These pilot resources are English-only.
- **Level 3:** lesson/unit packs are a future possibility, not currently published resources.

See [Quick Assign architecture](docs/QUICK_ASSIGN_ARCHITECTURE.md) for all fifteen assignment links and teacher criteria. The [Student Lab Sheet](https://lmdixon23.github.io/ai-playgrounds/student-lab.html) provides a general inquiry structure.

## Classroom delivery

Learner applets and Quick Assigns support English, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese, and Spanish. The Curriculum Map and Teacher Pack support English and Simplified Chinese; see the [full language matrix](docs/PUBLIC_SURFACE_LOCALE_MATRIX.md).

Use the [standalone downloads](https://lmdixon23.github.io/ai-playgrounds/downloads.html) to distribute individual HTML files for offline use. Students submit work through the teacher's usual system, not an AI Playgrounds backend. Software checks do not establish curriculum effectiveness or learning gains; see [Quality](QUALITY.md).
